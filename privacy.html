Privacy Policy

A straightforward explanation of how this website handles information.

In brief: This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or account registration.

Information we collect

SynopWave Innovations LLC does not collect personal information through this website automatically. Our hosting provider may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. That processing is governed by the provider's own policies.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, and message. We use it only to respond, provide requested support, maintain business records, and protect our legal interests.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, when legally required, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention

We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, including support, business, security, and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have sent us, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

Children's privacy

This company website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or legal obligations change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
