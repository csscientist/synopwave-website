Food Trust Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Food Trust mobile app uses, sends, and stores information.

In brief: Food Trust has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or location tracking. App data is primarily stored on your device.

Who operates Food Trust

Food Trust is operated by SynopWave Innovations LLC. This policy applies to the Food Trust mobile app.

Camera access

Food Trust uses your device's camera only to scan product barcodes. Camera access is not used for advertising, analytics, or location tracking.

Requests sent to Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode, search for a product, or request product details, Food Trust sends the barcode number, product search, or related lookup request to Open Food Facts. Product images may also be loaded from Open Food Facts or its associated image services.

Open Food Facts and its service providers may process these requests according to their own privacy practices. Internet requests generally include technical information needed to return a response, such as your IP address.

Information stored on your device

Food Trust stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Favorite products
  • Recent products and recent searches
  • Cached product details
  • Shopping sessions
  • Product information you submit locally in the app

This information is not synchronized to a SynopWave account or cloud service. You can remove locally stored information through available controls in the app or by uninstalling Food Trust.

Services Food Trust does not use

Food Trust currently has no user accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or location tracking.

Sale of personal information

SynopWave Innovations LLC does not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

Food Trust may change in the future. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and will revise the effective date shown above.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email admin@synopwaveinnovations.com or visit synopwaveinnovations.com.
