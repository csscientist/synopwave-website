Food Trust

Evidence-based grocery shopping.

Food Trust helps people understand food ingredients, their sources and relevant processing, with halal compatibility as its first assessment focus. It distinguishes supporting evidence from information that is still unknown.

Our private review workflow uses an operator-authorized Food Trust Gmail mailbox to send product questions and match replies for review. Shoppers do not need to connect their Gmail accounts. A received reply does not automatically approve a product.
